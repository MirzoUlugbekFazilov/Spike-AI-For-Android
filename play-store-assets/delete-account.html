Delete Your Spike AI Account

This page explains how to delete your Spike AI account and the personal data associated with it, as required by Google Play.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Spike AI and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete your account and data:

Email: sami@londonmedialounge.co.uk
Subject: "Delete my Spike AI account"

We process deletion requests within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your name and email address
  • Your account / user ID
  • Your focus sessions, goals, and progress data

No data is retained after deletion, except where we are required to keep limited records to comply with legal obligations.
