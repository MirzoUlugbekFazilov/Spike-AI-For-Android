## Full description (max 4000 chars) — edit freely
Spike AI helps you take control of your time. Set a goal, start a focus
session, and let Spike keep you on track with smart, AI-driven nudges that
adapt to how you actually work — not a one-size-fits-all timer.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Focus sessions — Start a distraction-free session and stay locked in until
  your goal is done.
• AI guidance — Get personalised prompts and encouragement that adapt to your
  habits and pace.
• Sweat mode — Use your camera to track movement and form during quick workout
  breaks, so you stay sharp and energised.
• Progress tracking — See your streaks and sessions over time and build a
  routine that sticks.
• Clean, modern design — A simple, focused interface that gets out of your way.

WHY SPIKE AI
Most focus apps just count down. Spike AI actually responds to you — helping
you start, stay, and finish, then rewarding momentum so the habit compounds.

Whether you're studying, working, training, or just trying to stop scrolling,
Spike AI turns intention into action.

Start your first focus session today.
